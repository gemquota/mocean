SIMULATION NOTESFOUNDATION PASS
01

Self-directed exploration

Autonomous steering blends cruising, drifting, exploring, darting, turning and recovery. It continuously chooses its next movement without user input.

02

Body-relative thrust

Forward propulsion is strongest. Reverse, lateral and vertical movement are weaker. Forces are applied through the creature's current orientation.

03

Momentum has a vote

Inertia, water drag, buoyancy and finite angular response shape movement. Intent changes never teleport the body or overwrite its velocity.

04

One source of truth

All four renderers consume the same pose and animation state. Visual differences cannot quietly become physics differences.
