Phase 1 · True 3D locomotion foundation

6 / 6 tasks confirmed · gate pending

Phase goal: establish a coherent 3D attitude and independent translation channels. Dependencies: Phase 0 coordinate and force conventions. Acceptance: quaternion stability, world-vertical lift, body-local propulsion and multi-axis travel.

Subgoal 1.1 Persistent quaternion attitude frame
Build a coherent 3D orientation frame with quaternion-based attitude.
Subgoal 1.2 Decouple translation forces from attitude control
Separate thrust / lift / lateral propulsion from pitch, yaw and roll control.
Subgoal 1.3 Establish the full translation basis
Support forward, reverse, lateral, vertical and diagonal travel.
Subgoal 1.4 Fixed-camera depth, scale and visibility
Make depth travel visibly change perspective scale on the fixed camera.
Subgoal 1.5 Distance blur and fade
Add distance-based blur and fade near one-quarter apparent size.
Subgoal 1.6 Natural return to the visible habitat
Guide the fish back toward the habitat centre with world-space orientation and body-forward thrust.

Phase 2 · Elementary movement primitives

Queued

Teach each movement independently before combining them.

Straight swimming, acceleration, coasting and braking.
Controlled pitch-up and pitch-down independently of translation.
Controlled yaw turns with bounded angular acceleration.
True axial roll around the fish's local head-to-tail axis, independent of pitch and yaw.
Validate each primitive alone with local-axis visual cues.

Phase 3 · Basic composite motions

Queued

Combine a small number of primitives into clear, purposeful manoeuvres.

Proper dive: pitch nose down first, then accelerate forward along the new heading.
Climb using the same orient-then-propel sequence.
Combine turns with acceleration, coasting and braking while preserving momentum.
Combine axial roll with a turn or glide without unwanted yaw spin.
Test smooth transitions between composites and calm cruising.

Phase 4 · Complex manoeuvre arrangements

Queued · later milestone

Long sequences with momentum, counter-steering and changing orientation.

Backwards drift while the head turns against current travel direction.
Carry momentum through a curved reversal and controlled loop-the-loop.
Combine dive, braking, reorientation and recovery into one continuous manoeuvre.
Build full 3D compound sequences with axial roll as a deliberate component, not a default spin.

Phase 5 · Autonomous natural behaviour

Queued

Intentional movement, not random steering noise.

Implement fish-level persistent behavioural intent and state transitions. This is runtime fish behaviour, not the project-planning hierarchy.
Blend cruise, explore, dart, glide, turn and recovery states.
Plan smooth trajectories with anticipation and obstacle margins.
Tune energy, rest and movement variation for a convincing rhythm.

Phase 6 · Environment & water surface

Explicitly deferred

Only starts after locomotion and behaviour are accepted.

Add sparse bubbles with buoyant rise and size variation.
Add tiny plankton / detritus with layered drift and parallax.
Add occasional drifting sticks with believable passive motion.
Build a visible water surface with travelling ripples and swells.
Add a basic wind field driving small waves and intermittent chop.

Phase 7 · Integration & polish

Queued

Unify motion, visuals, performance and deployment.

Tune lighting, depth cues, silhouette and material response.
Profile simulation and rendering; stabilise fixed-step behaviour.
Run regression tests and verify public Pages deployment.
Complete final visual and behavioural acceptance review.

Phase 2 · Elementary movement primitives Queued · not started

Goal: validate isolated movement primitives before combining them. Review: straight swimming, acceleration/coasting/braking, independent pitch and yaw, true axial roll, local-axis visual cues and regression preservation. Evidence required: per-primitive tests, measured trajectories, visual demonstrations and regression comparison against the accepted Phase 1 baseline. Dependency: explicit Phase 1 gate approval.

Phase 3 · Basic composite motions Queued · not started

Goal: combine validated primitives into purposeful manoeuvres. Review: orient-then-propel dives and climbs, turns with momentum, roll combinations and smooth transitions. Evidence required: sequence-level kinematic checks, continuous visual playback and regression tests. Dependency: accepted Phase 2 primitives.

Phase 4 · Complex manoeuvre arrangements Queued · not started

Goal: produce longer manoeuvre sequences with coherent momentum and counter-steering. Review: backwards drift, curved reversal, loop-the-loop, dive/brake/reorient/recover and deliberate 3D compound motion. Evidence required: bounded angular/linear dynamics, trajectory continuity and visual review of complete sequences. Dependency: accepted Phase 3 composites.

Phase 5 · Autonomous natural behaviour Queued · not started

Goal: make movement emerge from persistent fish-level intent rather than random steering. Review: behavioural state transitions, anticipation, trajectory planning, cruise/explore/dart/glide/recovery blending, energy and rest rhythms. Evidence required: repeatable behaviour tests plus extended observation showing varied but coherent activity. Dependency: validated locomotion primitives and composites.

Phase 6 · Environment & water surface Deferred

Goal: add environmental context only after locomotion and behaviour are accepted. Review: bubbles, plankton/detritus, drifting objects, surface ripples and wind-driven chop. Evidence required: passive environmental motion that does not obscure or destabilise the fish's locomotion. Dependency: accepted Phase 5 behaviour; this phase is deliberately deferred.

Phase 7 · Integration & polish Queued · not started

Goal: integrate the accepted simulation, rendering and delivery into a robust public experience. Review: lighting/material/depth quality, fixed-step stability, performance on ordinary hardware, regression suite, deployment and final behavioural acceptance. Evidence required: profiling, full regression results, deployment verification and final user review. Dependency: preceding accepted phases.
